# Contract: The Pattern Generator

## Store routes (shopper-facing; the `pattern_generator` switch gates them)

| Route | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `GET /how-complicated` | The dial (R4). Query: `r` (a recipe code, optional), `seed` (a 32-bit override, optional), `style` (`polo` or `button-down`, optional). An invalid `r` falls back to the default (step 20, polo, seed 1). Switch off: the store's 404. |
| `GET /how-complicated/art/[code]` | `image/svg+xml` for a valid code at or under `maxDial`. A made shirt's stored art, otherwise freshly generated. `nosniff`. Switch off, or an invalid or beyond code: 404. |
| `/shirts` and `/` | While the switch is on, a call to action: "How complicated can it get?" → `/how-complicated`. |
| `/shirts/<slug>` of a dial shirt | The shirt's own page. While it's one of a kind, the page depends on who's looking: see "One-of-a-kind shirts in the store" below. Mass-produced, it's an ordinary shirt page. |

**The dial page's contract with the browser**:
- The step list holds no score. The slider's `aria-valuenow` is the step (0–101), and its
  `aria-valuetext` is the tier name.
- Visible text, links and the document title contain no score.
- The price, the badge and Make it mine are `data-steady`.

## Store action

- **`makeItMine(code)`**:
  - **A guest** (owner review, R14) is redirected to `/sign-in?next=/how-complicated?r=<code>`.
    Nothing is made.
  - **Refused, with a store-voice message, when**:
    - the switch is off
    - the code isn't a recipe
    - it's the beyond step, or the price can't be computed
    - a catalog rule would break
  - **Otherwise**: `materialize(recipe, { origin: "dial", makerUserId })`, then redirect to
    `/shirts/<slug>`. The same recipe always lands on the same shirt. A shirt with no maker is
    claimed. Someone else's stays theirs.

## One-of-a-kind shirts in the store (owner review)

| Where | For its maker | For anyone else |
|---|---|---|
| `/shirts/<slug>` | The price, plus a "+ $X Uniquity Surcharge™ per shirt" note while the switch is on. Made-to-order stock line. Size picker and Add to Cart. No fake crowd, no "just bought". | The art, name, badge and price, then "One of a kind, and it's someone else's." No size picker, no Add to Cart. A guest also gets a sign-in link back to this page. |
| `addItem` / `updateItem` (a raise) | As any shirt | Refused: `NOT_YOURS` |
| Cart audit (every cart view, and `placeOrder`) | Kept | Taken out with reason `unique` |
| Cart, checkout, receipt, history | A "Uniquity Surcharge™ × N" line: the amount × N. It's in the total. | — |

**`placeOrder`** (R15): `surchargeUsd` comes from the live lines and settings, inside the
transaction. `expectedTotalUsd` must equal shirts + surcharges + add-ons, or `PRICE_CHANGED`.
`order_items.surcharge_usd` and `orders.surcharge_usd` keep what was charged.

## Console (behind `requireAdmin`, and calm)

| Route | Shows | Actions |
|---|---|---|
| `/admin/shirts/generate?score=&style=&seed=` | The preview (an admin art route image), the counted score, tier, MSRP, and a prefilled name. The score box has a slider spaced like the dial (R17). | `saveGeneratedShirt` |
| `GET /admin/generator/art/[code]` | SVG for admins (it works with the switch off). Others: 404. | — |
| `/admin/shirts` | Origin marks: "Generated by you" or "Made by a shopper". Filter `?origin=catalog\|owner\|dial` | — |
| `/admin/shirts/[id]` (a dial shirt) | Its maker (linked to the customer). `ListedControl`: "Mass-produced" or "One of a kind" (R18) | `setShirtListed` |
| `/admin/settings` | The 14th switch, "Pattern generator", and the 15th, "Uniquity Surcharge", each with one plain line. The surcharge amount (R15). | `setSwitch` (keys `patternGenerator`, `uniquitySurcharge`), `saveSurcharge` |

- **`saveGeneratedShirt`**:
  - **Fields**: `score` (an integer from 0 to `maxDial`), `style`, `seed`, `name` (1–80
    characters).
  - **Refused when**:
    - outside the range, or the price can't be computed
    - the recipe is already a shirt (the message links to it)
    - the name is out of bounds
    - a catalog rule would break
  - **Otherwise**: materialize with `origin: "owner"` (resting, listed), set the name, and redirect
    to `/admin/shirts/<id>?saved=1`.
- **`setShirtListed`**:
  - **Fields**: `id`, `listed` (`"1"` or `"0"`).
  - **Refused when**: the shirt isn't a dial shirt.
  - **Otherwise**: it saves only `listed`.

- **`saveSurcharge`** (owner review):
  - **Fields**: `amountUsd` (dollars and cents, `0.00` or more).
  - **Refused when**: it isn't an amount.
  - **Otherwise**: it saves `uniquity_surcharge_usd`. Carts follow on their next look.

All three join the 008 door sweep (guests and shoppers are refused, with nothing changed).

## Library (pure, `src/lib/pattern/`)

| Function | Contract |
|---|---|
| `generate({ score, style, seed }, { bland })` | `{ svg, count }`. Deterministic, byte for byte, and `count === score`. `0 ≤ score ≤ ART_MAX_SCORE` (24,000 after the owner review, R13). |
| `countPattern(structure)` | ⌊points ÷ 10⌋, plus 5 for each extra color, plus 10 for each extra layer (owner review, R1). Monotonic in additions. |
| `scoreAtStep(step, maxDial)` | Non-decreasing over 0–100. 101 means beyond. |
| `maxPriceableScore(msrp)` | The largest score `msrpUsd` can price. |
| `encodeRecipe` / `decodeRecipe` | They round-trip. `decodeRecipe` returns null for anything else. The code contains no digits of the score in order. |
| `recipeKey(recipe)` | `v1:<style>:<score>:<seed>`, server-only. |
| `nameFor(recipe, bland)` | Deterministic, from Dan's word banks, with no digits. |
